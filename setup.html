Set up a really helpful tool

Runs only in your browser. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere except to GitHub to test the token.

Create at github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new. Repository access: only the vault repo. Permission: Contents = Read and write. Full steps in docs/SETUP.md.

At least 10 characters. A short sentence works well, e.g. "blue tram to Karlsfeld 42". The encrypted token is public, so a weak password could be guessed.

Your new config.js

Replace the whole content of config.js in your app repo with this, then commit. The site updates in about a minute.

Close this tab afterwards. You can delete the token text from your clipboard history too.
